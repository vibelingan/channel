heading: Privacy notice
intro: >-
  Diversity Technology Limited runs this website. This notice explains what we
  collect when you use it, why, where it is processed, how long we keep it,
  and how to ask us to see, correct or delete it.

sections:
  - heading: What we collect
    items:
      - title: Project form
        body: >-
          Company, contact name, email, optional WhatsApp number, product
          details, quantity and any files you upload. We use them to review
          your project and reply with a feasibility review or quotation.
      - title: Accounts
        body: >-
          Your email and password if you create an account. The password is
          stored only as a one-way hash.
      - title: AI assistant
        body: >-
          Your questions, the assistant's answers and the products it showed.
          Chatting alone does not send anything to our sales team: only an
          inquiry you review and confirm does, with the name, email, company
          and requirement you entered.
      - title: Technical records
        body: >-
          To limit abuse we keep a keyed hash of your IP address, not the
          address itself, together with request times.

  - heading: How the assistant uses AI
    items:
      - title: AI answers
        body: >-
          The assistant is an AI. It answers from our approved public
          information and can make mistakes. Prices, minimum order quantities
          and lead times are always confirmed by our team.
      - title: AI model provider
        body: >-
          To write an answer, recent messages from your conversation are sent
          to the AI model service we use. Email addresses and phone numbers you
          type are removed before anything is sent.

  - heading: Where it is processed
    items:
      - title: Hosting
        body: >-
          The website, forms, accounts and the assistant run on Tencent Cloud
          in mainland China (Shanghai region).
      - title: Who receives it
        body: >-
          Our own team, and the service providers named in this notice for
          the purposes described. We do not sell personal data.

  - heading: How long we keep it
    items:
      - title: Inquiries and projects
        body: >-
          Kept as business records for as long as we need them to follow up
          and to keep our records.
      - title: Conversations
        body: >-
          Conversations about our products, services or an inquiry are kept as
          business records. Conversations with no product or business content
          may be deleted earlier.
      - title: On request
        body: >-
          You can ask us to delete your conversation, inquiry, project or
          account at any time.

  - heading: Your browser
    items:
      - title: Assistant
        body: >-
          The assistant keeps your conversation key and any unsent inquiry in
          this tab only, so a refresh or a click to another page continues the
          same conversation. Closing the tab clears it.
      - title: Accounts and inquiry list
        body: >-
          If you sign in, or add products to an inquiry list, the browser keeps
          them until you sign out or clear the list.
      - title: No tracking
        body: We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

  - heading: Your choices and contact
    items:
      - title: Your rights
        body: >-
          You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object
          to how we use it. We may need to confirm it is you before we act.
      - title: Contact
        body: Email sales@supplychainsai.com with the word "Privacy" in the subject.
